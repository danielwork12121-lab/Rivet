Bounty size

The app suggests a bounty from the client’s own ROI. The client can move it up or down before publishing.

Fair pledges

Each backer’s suggested pledge scales with what the robot saves them, so a small crew isn’t asked to match a large one.

pledge ∝ that client’s savings

Opt-in pooling

Pooling is off by default. Anonymous clients get ROI and matches but can’t start or back a bounty until they sign in and opt in.
